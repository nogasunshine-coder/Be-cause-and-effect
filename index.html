Welcome to Our City!

Greeters¹ are people who welcome tourists² to their city and show them some of its special places.

Greeters are volunteers. They love their city very much, so they want to show it to tourists. For free³!

Each greeter takes four to six tourists on a walking tour⁴. Morning tours start at 9:00. Evening tours start at 7:00. The tourists spend two or three hours with the greeters.

The greeters take the tourists to the places they like best. They show them small shops or cafés, music clubs, art galleries, and other special places. They tell them stories about old houses and the people who live or used to live there.

"Greeters are nice and friendly people," says Mr. Marks, a tourist. "They make me feel at home in the city I visit. It's like taking a walk with a friend."

If you and your family visit a city with a greeter, here's some advice: Don't tip⁵ the greeter after you take the tour. Greeters are volunteers. They work for free.
